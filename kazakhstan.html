Did you know?

  • Kazakhstan is the largest landlocked country in the world, bigger than all of Western Europe.
  • The first person in space, Yuri Gagarin, was launched in 1961 from the Baikonur Cosmodrome in Kazakhstan, the world's first and largest space launch site.
  • Scientists believe the wild apple forests near Almaty are the ancestors of the apples we eat today.
  • The capital moved from Almaty to Astana in 1997.

Local Food

Beshbarmak

The national dish: boiled meat served over wide flat noodles with onion broth. Its name means "five fingers" because it is traditionally eaten by hand.

Baursak

Golden puffs of fried dough, served with tea and at almost every celebration.

Manti

Large steamed dumplings filled with spiced meat and onion.

Where to visit

Charyn Canyon

A huge red-rock canyon more than 150 km long, often compared to the Grand Canyon. Its most famous part is called the Valley of Castles.

Bayterek Tower, Astana

A 97-meter tower in the capital with a golden glass sphere on top. It stands for a legendary tree of life, and you can go up for a view over the city.

Big Almaty Lake

A turquoise mountain lake high in the Tien Shan mountains, about 2,500 meters above sea level, close to the city of Almaty.
